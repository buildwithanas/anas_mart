Frequently asked questions

Standard shipping takes 5–7 business days, Express 2–3 days, and Overnight arrives the next business day. You'll get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.
We accept returns within 30 days of delivery, no restocking fee. Items should be unused and in original packaging. Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–7 business days of us receiving the return.
We accept all major debit and credit cards via Stripe, PayPal, and direct bank transfer. Additional local payment methods may be available depending on your country at checkout.
If your order hasn't shipped yet, contact support right away and we'll do our best to change or cancel it. Once it's marked "Shipped," we're unable to make changes — you're welcome to return it once it arrives.
